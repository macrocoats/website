Our approach

Engineered for performance

We combine engineering, chemistry and process understanding to deliver measurable improvements on the shop floor.

Each programme starts from the line: the substrate, the water, the paint system and the production conditions it has to work within.

01

Application-specific chemistry

Formulations designed around your substrate, water quality, paint system and production conditions.

02

Low-temperature processing

Zinc phosphating effective at 45–55 °C. Lower energy consumption and better operating economics.

03

Chromium-free technologies

Environmentally responsible passivation for a safer workplace and a sustainable future.

04

Automation-ready processes

Automatic dosing systems, online pH monitoring and process control for consistent quality.

05

Reliable surface preparation

Stable bath performance: lower sludge, longer bath life, consistent coating weight and fewer rejections.

06

Technical application support

On-site support, process optimisation, training and continuous improvement through a long-term partnership.

Surface preparation isn’t another process step. It’s the foundation of every durable finish.
