Family 01 · 06 products

Surface preparation

Build the coating on a clean, active and dimensionally controlled substrate.

UNKLEAN-ALSurface prep

Aluminium Degreaser

Alkaline · pH 10–11.5 · Low foam

Non-etching cleaner for stamping oils and machining coolants without attacking the native aluminium oxide layer.

Best fit
Extrusions, die-cast parts, HVAC and anodising lines.

UNKLEAN-SPSurface prep

Heavy-Duty Degreaser

Alkaline · pH 11–13 · High load

Removes forming oils, neat cutting fluids and anti-rust compounds from production metalwork.

Best fit
Stamped steel, machined parts and heavy fabrications.

UNISOLVE H3Surface prep

Heavy-Duty De-rust

Inhibited HCl · High strength

Fast removal of heavy rust, mill scale and weld heat scale with controlled base-metal attack.

Best fit
Structural steel, hot-rolled sections and rework.

UNISOLVESurface prep

Inhibited De-rust

H₂SO₄ / H₃PO₄ · Inhibited

Light-to-medium rust removal with lower hydrogen-embrittlement risk and a mildly passivated finish.

Best fit
Cold-rolled and high-tensile steel.

UNKLEAN 3 IN 1Surface prep

Three-in-One Treatment

Clean · De-rust · Phosphate

Single-stage chemistry for light-duty jobs where a full multi-tank pretreatment line is not practical.

Best fit
Job shops, maintenance and field fabrication.

UNIKSTRIPSurface prep

Paint & Scale Stripper

Alkaline · pH 13–14 · 60–80°C

Strips cured powder coat, liquid paint and scale while preserving the underlying metal geometry.

Best fit
Rejected parts, production jigs and conveyor hooks.

Family 02 · 07 products

Phosphating

Control coating weight, crystal structure and the foundation for downstream paint or oil.

UNIKOAT ZN-NIPhosphating

Zinc-Nickel Phosphate

Fine crystal · 2–4 µm · 40–55°C

Premium fine-crystal zinc-nickel system developed for high paint adhesion under CED and KTL.

Best fit
Automotive body-in-white and Tier-1 stamped parts.

UNIKOAT LT450Phosphating

Zinc Phosphate — Hot

40–60°C · 4–10 g/m²

Standard-temperature conversion coating for reliable paint adhesion in seven-stage production lines.

Best fit
Automotive stampings, appliances and equipment.

UNIKOAT LT700Phosphating

Zinc Phosphate — RT Heavy

Ambient · 7–15 g/m²

Room-temperature heavy coating that cuts heating demand while retaining robust ferrous-metal protection.

Best fit
Energy-sensitive and heavy-engineering lines.

UNIKOAT LT450-RTPhosphating

Zinc Phosphate — RT Fine

Ambient · 2–5 g/m² · Fine crystal

Fine-crystal ambient system tuned for paint anchor profile on close-tolerance steel components.

Best fit
Powder coating and thin-wall stampings.

UNIKOAT LT2000Phosphating

Zinc Phosphate — Heavy

65–75°C · 15–25 g/m²

High-build zinc phosphate for maximum standalone corrosion protection in demanding service.

Best fit
Railways, coastal equipment and heavy engineering.

UNI-IRON PHOSPHATEPhosphating

Iron Phosphate

45–65°C · 0.3–1.0 g/m²

Economical amorphous conversion coating with low sludge generation for compact paint lines.

Best fit
Appliances, furniture, doors and light fabrication.

UNI-MANGANESE PHOSPhosphating

Manganese Phosphate

80–98°C · 5–30 g/m²

Dense oil-retaining crystal structure for wear resistance, break-in lubrication and anti-galling.

Best fit
Gears, shafts, railway components and firearms.

Family 03 · 05 products

Passivation & sealing

Extend corrosion performance with chrome and chrome-free conversion and post-treatment routes.

UNIPASS-AL CHROMEPassivation

Aluminium Passivation — Chrome

Cr⁶⁺ · Yellow iridescent · 200+ h

Self-healing chromate conversion for maximum corrosion performance and a strong base for paint.

Best fit
Aerospace, defence and high-spec hardware.

UNIPASS-AL NON CHROMEPassivation

Aluminium Passivation — Cr-Free

Zr/Ti · REACH · RoHS · EU ELV

Chrome-free conversion layer designed as a compliant drop-in route for export supply chains.

Best fit
Automotive aluminium, electronics and HVAC.

UNIPASS-ALPassivation

Aluminium Passivation — Standard

Clear film · 72–120 h

Cost-controlled clear conversion coating compatible with powder coat, liquid paint and lacquer.

Best fit
Profiles, hardware and general industrial aluminium.

UNIPASS-FE CHROMEPassivation

Iron Passivation — Chrome

Cr⁶⁺ · Post-phosphate · 200+ h

Self-healing post-phosphate sealer for higher corrosion resistance where chrome remains permitted.

Best fit
Severe service and non-ELV applications.

UNIPASS-FE NON CHROMEPassivation

Iron Passivation — Cr-Free

Zr/organic · 120–200 h

Compliant post-phosphate sealer with no hexavalent chromium and no required equipment change.

Best fit
European automotive and appliance supply chains.

Family 04 · 03 products

Bath auxiliaries

Stabilise crystal growth, accelerate conversion and protect finished parts through storage and transit.

UNIKCONDITIONERBath auxiliary

Titanium Conditioner

Ti colloid · pH 7–9

Pre-phosphate nucleation treatment for a tighter, finer and more uniform crystal structure.

Best fit
Zinc and manganese phosphate lines.

UNIKTONNERBath auxiliary

Phosphate Accelerator

Oxidising · NO₂⁻ / ClO₃⁻

Controls hydrogen evolution, shortens process time and supports uniform coating in complex geometry.

Best fit
High-throughput and blind-hole components.

UNIKGUARD RPIBath auxiliary

Rust Preventive Compound

Water-displacing · 6–12 months

Thin self-levelling protective film for storage, export and inter-process transit after treatment.

Best fit
Finished ferrous parts and post-phosphate storage.
